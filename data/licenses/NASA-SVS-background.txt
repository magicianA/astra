The background is the published faint-star layer, with bright Hipparcos/Tycho
foreground stars omitted by the source author. Astra preserves its native
16384x8192 spatial sampling. Offline conversion decodes linear EXR, applies a
per-channel display gain of 16 with x/(1+x) highlight compression, then encodes
8-bit sRGB PNG. Only the two polar rows are spatially averaged, in linear light.
There is no median filtering, sharpening, invented noise, AI detail or upscaling.
The renderer adds linear-light mipmaps, optional anisotropic sampling, restrained
colour saturation, display exposure, and atmospheric attenuation.

This is a visualization, not absolute radiance or a time-evolving catalogue.
Its faint stars and Galactic structure stay fixed; survey artefacts can remain.
Dynamic foreground stars and Solar System bodies are rendered independently.
Source and runtime SHA-256 hashes are in background/manifest.json.
