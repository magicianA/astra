This describes the archived Gaia EDR3 source and former background.
The current background/milky-way.png uses NASA SVS data; see NASA-SVS-background.txt.
The archived derived EDR3 map remains under the same CC BY-SA licence.
Changes by the Astra project: use the native 4000x2000 high-resolution map;
suppress compact stellar points with a 3-pixel median filter and longitude
wrapping; average the polar rows. No Gaussian blur or upscaling is applied.
Rendering builds linear-light mipmaps and applies restrained colour saturation,
display brightness, atmospheric attenuation, and a time-dependent celestial
orientation.
No endorsement by ESA, Gaia, DPAC or A. Moitinho is implied.

The map is a published visualization of integrated stellar brightness and colour,
not a calibrated radiance map. Processing details and SHA-256 hashes are recorded
in background/manifest.json. Its distant Galactic structure stays fixed in time;
it does not simulate the evolution of the Galaxy, dust, or embedded sources.
